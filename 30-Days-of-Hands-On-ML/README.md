# 30 Days of Hands-On Machine Learning 🚀

My 30-day intensive journey through Aurélien Géron's *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow (3rd Edition)* using VS Code.

Phase 1: Core Machine Learning & Data Pipelines (Days 1–15)

_This is the most critical half of the book. Master Part 1 completely, as it builds the bedrock of practical engineering._

|Day|Topic Coverage|Target Pages|Focus Action Items|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Day 1**|**Ch 1:** The Machine Learning Landscape|pp. 1 – 20|Learn ML types (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement).|
|**Day 2**|**Ch 1:** Testing & Validation|pp. 21 – 36|Understand overfitting, underfitting, and the bias/variance tradeoff.|
|**Day 3**|**Ch 2:** End-to-End Project (Part A)|pp. 37 – 58|Frame the California housing problem and structure train/test splits.|
|**Day 4**|**Ch 2:** Pipelines & Data Prep (Part B)|pp. 59 – 75|Master handling missing values, categorical attributes, and scaling.|
|**Day 5**|**Ch 2:** Evaluation & Cross-Validation|pp. 76 – 90|Run Grid Search and Randomized Search to tune hyperparameters.|
|**Day 6**|**Ch 3:** Classification & MNIST|pp. 91 – 105|Train a binary classifier on hand-written numbers.|
|**Day 7**|**Ch 3:** Confusion Matrix & Curves|pp. 106 – 122|Deconstruct Precision, Recall, F1-score, and ROC/AUC curves.|
|**Day 8**|**Ch 4:** Training Linear Models|pp. 123 – 144|Deep dive into the Normal Equation and Gradient Descent mechanics.|
|**Day 9**|**Ch 4:** Regularization & Logistic Models|pp. 145 – 164|Implement Ridge, Lasso, Elastic Net, and Softmax Regression.|
|**Day 10**|**Ch 5:** Support Vector Machines (SVMs)|pp. 165 – 186|Understand Linear SVM margin violations and the Polynomial Kernel trick.|
|**Day 11**|**Ch 6:** Decision Trees|pp. 187 – 202|Visualize tree boundaries, node impurities (Gini vs Entropy), and pruning.|
|**Day 12**|**Ch 7:** Ensemble Learning|pp. 203 – 222|Compare Voting classifiers, Bagging, and Random Forests.|
|**Day 13**|**Ch 7:** Boosting Methods|pp. 223 – 238|Execute AdaBoost and Gradient Boosting (XGBoost/LightGBM mechanics).|
|**Day 14**|**Ch 8:** Dimensionality Reduction|pp. 239 – 264|Target PCA (Principal Component Analysis) to fight the curse of dimensionality.|
|**Day 15**|**Ch 9:** Unsupervised Clustering|pp. 265 – 298|Explore K-Means, DBSCAN, and Anomaly Detection strategies.|
